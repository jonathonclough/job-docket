Hitting Submit logs the job with the office, emails a confirmation, and queues it to be entered into Tradify. Every job has to be picked from the list under "Job / vessel" before it can be submitted — say or type the job and tap the right one. If a brand-new job isn't in the list yet, it'll show up within the hour once it's in Tradify. Time not on a customer job? Tap "Workshop — non-billable" (or just say "workshop") and it goes into Tradify's timesheets as non-billable hours. Typing works offline; the mic, the job picker and Submit all need a connection, since they call out to the transcription, tidy-up, storage and email services — with no signal, keep what you've typed and hit Submit once you're back in range.
